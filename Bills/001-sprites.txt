BILL 001 - SPRITES (core: character template, arena, fx)

RULES FOR EVERY SPRITE
- PNG, pure white (#FFFFFF) pixels on transparent. No other colors, no anti-aliasing, no semi-transparent pixels. The game tints at runtime.
- Silhouettes only. Separate parts with a 1px transparent gap. No detail inside a silhouette.
- Everything faces RIGHT. The game mirrors it for left.
- Pivot is the point the game anchors to the world. It is listed per sprite.
- Save to the exact path listed. Do not rename.
- UI needs no sprites (drawn in code).

--------------------------------------------------
SPRITE: char_base
File: Sprites/Character/base.png
Size: 24x24
Pivot: bottom center (x=12, y=24). Feet rest on row 23.
Description: The universal stick figure, neutral standing pose, facing right. This is the template every character is built from.
- Total height 20px (rows 4-23). Rows 0-3 are headroom for raised arms and blurs.
- Head: filled circle, 6px wide, rows 4-9, centered on x=12 and nudged 1px right.
- Torso: 2px thick vertical line, 6px long, rows 10-15, touching the head.
- Arms: 2px thick, about 6px long, shoulders on row 11, hanging relaxed with a slight bend. Leave a 1px gap between each arm and the torso so they read as separate.
- Legs: 2px thick, 8px long, rows 16-23, joined at the hip, in a narrow inverted-V stance with the feet about 4px apart on row 23.
- The body mass sits inside an 8px wide box (columns 8-15). This is the 8x20 collision box. Limbs and effects may leave it during motion.
- It must read instantly as a stick figure. No face, no clothing.
