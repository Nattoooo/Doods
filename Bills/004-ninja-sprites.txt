BILL 004 - NINJA SPRITES

RULES FOR EVERY SPRITE
- Same rules as Bills 001-003: PNG, pure white (#FFFFFF) on transparent, no anti-aliasing, no semi-transparent pixels, silhouettes only, 1px transparent gaps between separate parts.
- Everything faces RIGHT. The game mirrors it for left and rotates it for dashes.
- Save to the exact path listed. Do not rename.
