BILL 002 - ANIMATIONS (core: character movement and fx)

RULES FOR EVERY ANIMATION
- Same rules as Bill 001: pure white on transparent PNG, no anti-aliasing, silhouettes only, 1px gaps between separate parts, facing right.
- Each animation is ONE horizontal strip. The frames are equal size, left to right, with no padding. Width = frame width x frame count. The frame counts below are exact because the game reads them.
- 24 FPS. Every frame lasts exactly one tick (1/24 s) unless it says HOLD.
- Smooth, clean, slightly exaggerated motion. Use squash and stretch, and 1px-gap-separated blur streaks in the same white.
- Character frames keep the pivot at the bottom center (x=12, y=24). The feet stay on row 23 whenever the character is on the ground. Keep the body mass in columns 8-15 unless the motion needs more.
- Build every character animation on the char_base design from Bill 001.

ANIMATION: char_idle
File: Sprites/Character/idle.png
Frames: 16 at 24x24 (384x24). Loops.
Description: A slow breathing loop. Frames 1-8: the head and shoulders lower 1px, the knees soften slightly and the arms drift out 1px. Frames 9-16: reverse back to the base pose. Ease in and out by spending the extra frames near the top and bottom of the motion. The feet never move. The first frame must match char_base.

ANIMATION: char_run
File: Sprites/Character/run.png
Frames: 8 at 24x24 (192x24). Loops. One cycle is 1/3 s.
Description: A fast run with a 2-3px forward lean and exaggerated stride. Frames 1 and 5 are the footfall contacts, where a foot lands and the body is 1px lower. The game spawns dust on those two frames. Frames 3 and 7 are the passing poses, where the body is 1px higher and one leg is tucked under. The arms pump opposite to the legs. On frames 2, 3, 6 and 7, add a 3-4px speed streak behind the trailing foot, separated by a 1px gap.

ANIMATION: char_jump_start

Frames: 3 at 24x24 (72x24). Plays once.
Description: Frame 1: a deep crouch, the body 4px lower, arms swung back. Frame 2: the push-off, the legs extending, the body stretching 2px taller, the arms swinging forward, the toes still touching row 23. ANIMATION: char_rise
File: Sprites/Character/rise.png

ANIMATION: char_apex

Description: The top of the jump. The character goes from a stretched pose (frame 1) to a compact tuck: the knees up, the arms spread wide for balance, the body about 16px tall (frame 4). Frame 4 should look weightless and work as a held pose.

ANIMATION: char_fall
File: Sprites/Character/fall.png

Description: Falling fast. The arms are thrown up and out, the legs dangle slightly apart, and the body is stretched. Two 3-4px vertical speed streaks above the head, separated by 1px gaps, shift each frame. It must loop seamlessly.

ANIMATION: char_wall_slide
